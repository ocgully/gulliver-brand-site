About

The short version of a long route.

I taught myself to program on projects nobody asked for. I wrote a game engine at 14, back when there was no Unity to skip that work, and started Nobis Productions in 1998 to keep building. Nobis Technologies followed in 2004: an osCommerce payment plugin wired to VeriSign, a suction-cup toll-transponder clip, and an order-fulfilment command center. It was the pre-Stripe, pre-app-store era, and every piece of it was built the hard way.

I studied computer science at the University of Central Florida and took a master's in interactive entertainment at its Florida Interactive Entertainment Academy. That put me in games. I worked at Midway Games on Wheelman, then at n-Space, where I shipped Marvel: Ultimate Alliance 2 on Wii and three Nintendo DS titles: Toy Story 3, James Bond 007: Blood Stone, and GoldenEye 007. In 2010 I was a tools engineer at id Software while Rage was in development.

At Disney Interactive I moved from release and gameplay engineering to lead architect, shipping mobile games including Where's My Mickey? and Inside Out Thought Bubbles, and architecting the Launchpad framework that around 90 developers across three teams built on. After that I directed engineering at Tap Slots, then led mobile and later the deals and community value streams at Slickdeals. Today I am a senior software engineering manager at DraftKings, leading 39+ engineers on the Sportsbook and Casino app, work that has carried $25M+ in incremental revenue.
