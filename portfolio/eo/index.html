Eō

A virtual telepresence and sightseeing videogame that rewarded players for virtually visiting and exploring geographic points of interest using a treadmill or stationary bike as the controller. The project aimed to combat obesity and inactivity by making exercise social, fun, educational, and a view-port to the outside world. Also known as Walk Around the Globe and WAG.

CompanyEndless Train LLC
Years2004–2012
PlatformsPCTreadmillStationary Bike
TypeGraveyard

What was built

Led development of this innovative fitness game concept that combined 3D scanning technology with exercise equipment to create immersive virtual travel experiences. Features: Social fitness through virtual telepresence with friends; Exploration of 3D-scanned real-world locations; Educational content about visited destinations; Game modes including 'Run! Zombies!' for added excitement; Virtual trainer to guide users through proper exercise techniques; Achievement system with passport stamps for visited locations; Compatible with existing treadmills and stationary bikes. Built with: 3D scanning technology, Virtual reality, Exercise equipment integration, Social networking, Geolocation data.

Why it ended

Kickstarter campaign was unsuccessful and the technology required for high-quality 3D scanning of cities was prohibitively expensive at the time.
